Welcome home

A Bible-based holiness church, right here in Bahrain

We are part of the Deeper Christian Life Ministry, a family of believers gathered to know Christ deeply, live holy, and love one another. Whether you are new to the Kingdom or searching for a church home, you are welcome exactly as you are.

"...your spiritual welfare is our concern."

Our services are held in English, with warm fellowship for families, youth, and children after every gathering.
